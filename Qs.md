## User-Perspective Questions

### Discover Business Ideas

1. “What business opportunities exist in smart agriculture that are not already overcrowded?”
2. “Find a business idea that helps small farmers reduce water usage without expensive equipment.”
3. “Create a business idea by combining renewable energy and cold-storage technology.”
4. “What problems do hospitals face that could be solved using existing sensor technology?”
5. “Find an overlooked business opportunity in waste management.”
6. “Suggest a business that combines AI with affordable healthcare devices.”
7. “What everyday problem could be solved by combining two unrelated technologies?”
8. “Generate a business idea for rural communities using low-cost technologies.”
9. “Find a profitable opportunity hidden between transportation and clean energy patents.”
10. “Suggest a business idea that could start small and scale internationally.”

### Improve an Idea

11. “How can I make this smart irrigation idea more unique and commercially valuable?”
12. “What existing technologies can strengthen my idea for an affordable flood-warning system?”
13. “Which customer group would benefit most from this invention?”
14. “What is missing from my idea compared with existing solutions?”
15. “How could this idea be cheaper, easier to manufacture, or easier to adopt?”
16. “Suggest three different versions of this idea for consumers, businesses, and governments.”
17. “What industries could use this technology besides agriculture?”
18. “Turn this technical invention into a realistic business model.”

### Evaluate an Idea

19. “Which existing patents are most similar to my idea?”
20. “How is my idea different from the closest existing inventions?”
21. “What parts of this idea may create patent or competition risks?”
22. “What technical gap does this idea address?”
23. “Is this idea solving a real customer problem or only combining technologies?”
24. “What would be the biggest reason this business might fail?”
25. “What evidence supports the demand for this type of product?”
26. “What should I build first to test whether customers want it?”

## Best Signature Question

> “Find an overlooked business opportunity by combining two unrelated patent clusters. Explain the customer problem, proposed product, target market, revenue model, closest existing patents, technical difference, and the smallest version I could build first.”

## Example Unique User Query

> “Create a business idea by combining biodegradable electronics with precision agriculture. The solution should help small farms monitor soil conditions, cost less than existing systems, produce minimal waste, and have a clear difference from existing patents. Show the target customers, revenue model, closest patents, risks, and first prototype.”
